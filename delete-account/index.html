How to delete your HomePot account

HomePot is a shared expenses app for couples, roommates and families. This page explains how to delete your HomePot account and its data, what is deleted and what stays in the groups you share with other people.

Delete your account in the app

The quickest way is from the app itself. It only takes a minute:

  1. Open HomePot and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Settings tab and scroll down to the Danger zone section.
  3. Under Delete account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete my account again.

Your account is deleted as soon as you confirm. You will be signed out and this cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your membership in all of your groups.
  • Groups where you are the only member, with everything in them: expenses, categories, recurring expenses, monthly closings and receipt photos.

Groups you share with other people

Groups that still have other members are not deleted. You are removed from them and, if you were the owner, another member becomes the owner.

The expenses you recorded in those groups, including any receipt photos attached to them, remain visible to the other members as part of the group's shared history, so their past months and balances stay correct. They are no longer linked to your profile.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

If you have HomePot Pro, your subscription is billed by Apple or Google, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel it, so cancel it yourself, ideally before you delete your account:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings app > your name > Subscriptions > HomePot > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > HomePot > Cancel subscription.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, send an email to homepotapp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your HomePot account, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will process your request within 30 days and reply to confirm once your account has been deleted. If the request does not come from the account's email address, we may ask you to verify that the account is yours.

Good to know

  • Deletion is immediate and permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account or its data.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or your data.
  • You can also delete some data without closing your account: remove your profile photo, leave a group or delete a group you own, all from the app.
