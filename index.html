Household questions

Another question? The help page has all the answers.

Does the other person have to download the app?

Yes, to log their own expenses. You send them a link and they’re in the pot within a minute; if they don’t have the app, the link takes them to the store.

Is it for couples or for shared flats?

Both. Couples usually keep a shared pot; in a flat, everyone pays and it’s evened out at the end. HomePot does both.

Do we have to open a joint account?

No. HomePot doesn’t touch your bank: it keeps the pot’s balance from what each person puts in and what gets spent.

What happens if I reach 20 expenses in a month?

On the free plan each person logs up to 20 expenses a month. Once there, you can still see the pot and close the month; to log more, you move to Pro or wait for the next month.

We’re coming from Splitwise. What changes?

HomePot is made for a household: shared pot with balance, forecast, month close and scanner included, with no ads on the free plan.
