What changes on day one

Practical differences you'll feel as soon as you start.

Zero ads — forever
No banners, no interstitials, no cooldown timers. Our business model is an optional subscription, not your attention. Verified: there is not a single ad SDK in the HomePot codebase.
AI receipt scanner on the free tier
Snap a photo of any receipt and our AI extracts the total, each item, taxes, tips, merchant and currency — not just the total. Free with 10 scans a month; 50 a month on Pro.
Recurring bills + monthly close
Rent, internet, insurance — set them once and they repeat. At the end of the month, HomePot calculates the minimum transfers needed to zero everyone's balance, not a chain of small payments.
54 currencies + AI reads any receipt
54 currencies selectable in the app, and the AI scanner detects any ISO 4217 currency directly from a receipt image. Auto-converts at today's exchange rate.

HomePot isn't offline-first yet

If offline expense entry is a hard requirement, HomePot currently needs a network connection (it syncs live). Tricount and the older Splitwise client both handle offline better right now. We're working on it, but we'd rather tell you upfront than let you find out on a plane.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my Splitwise data?

CSV import is on the roadmap. You can export your Splitwise group as CSV today and we'll ship an importer soon. Drop us a line at homepotapp@gmail.com and we'll notify you the day it lands.

Is HomePot really free?

Yes. The free plan includes 1 group with unlimited members, 20 expenses per person per month, 10 AI receipt scans per month, month close, recurring expenses and zero ads — forever. Pro (from €2.92/month on annual billing) gives you unlimited expenses, 50 scans a month, as many groups as you want, exports and advanced statistics, and one subscription covers everyone in your groups.

How is this different from the Splitwise comparison page?

This page is for people who already know they want to leave Splitwise. The full comparison is for people still deciding — it walks through every feature tier by tier with links to public sources.

Are the claims about Splitwise honest?
